## Method

The synthetic analysis used a linear mixed-effects model with random intercepts for participant and stimulus and
a random slope for condition. Models were fitted with restricted maximum likelihood (REML), Satterthwaite degrees
of freedom, and a documented software version. The model converged without a singular fit. We report marginal and
conditional R-squared and an intraclass correlation coefficient. There was no longitudinal dropout.

The analysis plan was preregistered at https://osf.io/demo-only before data inspection.
