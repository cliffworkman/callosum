## Engineering objective

Determine whether either tested configuration merits separately authorized
revised-0.6 integration and bounded end-to-end acceptance. Frontier screening
buys cheap exclusion and discovery; human review buys expensive promotion;
acceptance supplies a harder falsification opportunity. No layer is ground truth.

NO_FLAG != FAITHFUL. Absence of detected failure is not affirmative fidelity
evidence. Panel counts are summaries, not probabilities or majority-vote truth.
Raters are separately generated but may share training, benchmark, and
optimization-related errors. No IID claim is made. Codex is infrastructure and
methods engineer, never an additional semantic rater.

## Phase -1 and Freeze A

Qualify actual accounts/surfaces only with fabricated copying/structure packets
matching the intended input/output envelope. Record practical capabilities,
unknown limits, mechanical failures, and hidden-capture evidence. A surface that
cannot preserve blinding is TECHNICALLY_UNAVAILABLE for the blinded phase; this
is not semantic failure. No actual surface qualification is claimed here.

The eight scientific attentional roles precede model assignment: literal
fidelity; subject/population; relationships/endpoints/direction; null/mixed/
uncertain; unsupported additions/import; qualifiers/modality; proposition
reconstructability; skeptical review. After qualification, assign all qualified
surfaces in roster order, round-robin across roles. Preserve all roles, with at
least three raters and at most three roles per rater; otherwise amend explicitly.
Role reassignment ends before semantic execution.

Freeze A covers the q_AIB Arm-1/Arm-2 corpus, source boundaries, materiality,
labels, shared task, qualified roster, roles, prompts, transport-independent
content, missingness/retries, provenance, blinding, orchestration access and
interpretation. Arm 0 is retained as historical/mechanical evidence. Do not
freeze the human queue or exact challenge allocation at this stage.

Each rater provides candidate ID, FLAG/UNCERTAIN/NO_FLAG, and a short
source-anchored reason for FLAG/UNCERTAIN only. Preserve source-relative rules:
context resolves references without importing neighboring findings; quotation
does not excuse bad annotation; honest absence/uncertainty may be faithful;
query irrelevance is separate; unrelated independent omissions are not
automatically loss; raw output cannot silently repair the effective object.

Prefer UTF-8 text. Log lossless format, batching, parser, scheduling, or mechanical
availability changes. Never trim or repair scientific content. Batch changes
remain a behavioral-context limitation. A semantic change is an explicit new
amendment/cohort, not a transport adjustment.

## Private interstudy adaptation

Close Study 1 with a response or terminal technical disposition for every
planned cell. Only then may private orchestration inspect individual labels,
reasons, disagreements, alleged mechanisms, low-concern regions, possible
shared blind spots and provenance. Do not expose these to Cliff.

Use content and patterns to design the smallest defensible human challenge,
not merely FLAG-count sorting. For each element record:

Study-1 pattern/uncertainty -> falsifiable human question -> procedure -> decision.

The private amendment records findings, changes, rationale, access identities,
time, blinding and artifact hashes. A Cliff-safe version omits the findings and
selection basis. Orchestration must not certify a configuration or optimize
selection to make a preferred configuration win.

This is declared adaptive interstudy design. Within-study changes outside
predeclared branches require another amendment.

## Freeze B and human review

Before the first human case, freeze exact eligibility and construction,
identities/procedures/seeds, initial burden, severe/reread rules, sentinels,
later challenges, stopping/expansion/replacement rules, maximum burden and
visibility. Real Freeze B does not exist in this delivery.

Starting proposals for interstudy refinement: three initial low-concern cases
per configuration; deliberate rereads of PROMOTEs; two outside-discovery
unanimous-NO_FLAG samples plus two genuinely screen-independent random samples;
two relation-heavy q_AIB challenges; up to three distinct depression
null/mixed/uncertain/qualification challenges where unresolved. These are not
quotas frozen at A. Preserve independent sentinels, relation scrutiny, challenge
outside selection conditions and distinct unresolved value classes. Do not
consume the separate 0.7 held-out material or automatically run full depression
or built-environment batteries.

Human task: PROMOTE / REJECT / UNCERTAIN, with a short source-anchored note only
for the latter two. Open relevant detailed diagnostics only after a problem.
Capture session/time/reviewer provenance automatically. No mandatory eleven-
dimension ratings. Present source, effective representation, necessary raw
output and opaque ID; hide explicit arms, panel information, prior labels,
rankings, selection rationale and sentinel/control status. Representation form
may reveal type, so arm blinding is imperfect.

One fresh-human-confirmed severe scientific failure halts the tested
configuration only: null reversal, invented relation, population/construct
substitution, causal strengthening, consequential direction/modality/qualifier
loss or scientific unusability. A later-session source-first reread hides the
earlier judgment. Cosmetic defects do not trigger this rule. Disagreement is
unresolved evidence, not grounds to average away a concern.

Approximately 20 minutes is a continuous-attention safeguard, ending between
cases with a break; it is not an inferential threshold. Ceiling: 24 unique cases
per configuration, including rejected/nonqualifying/replacement cases, and at
most two ordinary reviews per case before another explicit amendment.

Broaden only for a named engineering uncertainty. A human-confirmed serious
panel miss is evidence of limited screen sensitivity: halt the failed
configuration and apply the frozen additional screen-independent scrutiny to
survivors. Four extra cases are the default expansion proposal. A clean sentinel
does not certify the remaining pool. Failed challenges are never replaced by
favorable cases.

Terminal statuses: HALT_CONFIGURATION, INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, or
ELIGIBLE_FOR_INTEGRATION_REVIEW, computed from genuine human evidence at actual
execution. Both configurations may qualify; no comparative winner is required.
No automatic exhaustive fallback: a new amendment must establish its necessity
and address attention cost and prior exposure.

## Remaining authorization boundaries

This implementation authorizes no reading of real observations or the sealed
mapping, real queue generation, semantic screening, genuine human-label
generation, production changes, staging or committing. Surface submission is
not implemented. Capability capture and all lifecycle tests use synthetic
material; the demo's generated labels and outcomes have no scientific status.

The dependency remains two-study adjudication -> configuration decision ->
separately authorized integration -> separately authorized bounded end-to-end
acceptance -> only if READY, R_0_6 freeze. No 0.7 semantic execution.

Residual risks: shared frontier errors, role/model confounding, opaque surface
versions, batching effects, adaptive selection, imperfect form blinding, source
ambiguity, and single-human fatigue/drift. Neither hashes nor synthetic tests
remove these limits. The conclusion can only be "worth the next harder test."
