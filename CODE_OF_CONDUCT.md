# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Callosum a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
background or identity, and to act in ways that foster an open, welcoming, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Behavior that contributes to a positive environment includes: empathy and kindness; respect for differing
opinions, viewpoints, and experiences; gracefully giving and accepting constructive feedback; and focusing on
what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes: harassment of any kind; insulting, derogatory, or demeaning comments; personal
or political attacks; publishing others' private information without permission; and other conduct that could
reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This Code applies within all project spaces and when an individual is representing the project in public.
Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the repository maintainer (via a private channel or by
contacting the repository owner). Maintainers will review reports in good faith, respect the privacy and
security of the reporter, and may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, and contributors that
violate this Code.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
